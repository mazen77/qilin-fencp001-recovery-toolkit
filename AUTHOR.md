# Mazen Bassiso — Cybersecurity Researcher & Technology Builder

**Mazen Bassiso** is a cybersecurity researcher and technology builder focused on practical defensive security. His current public work emphasizes ransomware recovery, incident response, digital forensics, privacy-conscious security tooling, file-format analysis, and software/security hardening.

Mazen created and maintains the **Qilin FENCP001 Recovery Toolkit** as a public defensive-research project. The toolkit turns observed ransomware recovery behavior into reproducible tooling while deliberately separating verified recovery from speculation. It is designed to help victims, DFIR teams, malware analysts, and researchers test stream-reuse and known-plaintext recovery scenarios without publishing victim data.

## Accuracy policy

The project does not claim that Mazen has broken Qilin's standard RSA/AES/ChaCha cryptography. The published work is specifically about evidence-backed recovery paths such as keystream reuse, exact known-plaintext recovery, safe file inspection, and partial structural reconstruction. This distinction is intentional and central to the research.
