## Launch announcement — English

I’m open-sourcing the **Qilin FENCP001 Recovery Toolkit**, a defensive ransomware-recovery research project I developed around an undocumented `FENCP001` file format and stream-reuse recovery scenarios.

The project focuses on what can actually be verified: safe file inspection, RAR4 reconstruction, aligned stream-reuse analysis, exact-pair keystream derivation, and privacy-safe incident-response reporting. It does **not** pretend to break RSA/AES/ChaCha when the required weakness or plaintext is not present.

My goal is to make practical ransomware recovery research easier to reproduce, challenge, and improve publicly.
